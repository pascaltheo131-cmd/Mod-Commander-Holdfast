[h1]Commander — command your own line of bots[/h1]

[b]Server-side mod for Holdfast: Nations At War.[/b] Players install nothing: it works for PC, Xbox and PlayStation players alike. No telemetry, no statistics, no network calls.

Every officer can raise his own line of AI soldiers (bots) and lead it in battle with the game's own order wheel, voice phrases or chat commands. Commands work in English and in French.

[h2]Infantry lines[/h2]
[list]
[*]Spawn as [b]Infantry Officer[/b] and type [b]!line[/b] (or [b]!line 15[/b]; optional prefix, class and uniform — e.g. [i]!line GDE Guard 15 2[/i]).
[*]Type it before the raise opens (shown at round start): you are registered and your line is raised as soon as raising opens.
[*]Your line arrives with a flag bearer, a drummer or fifer and a medic when it is large enough.
[*]Game orders: Form Line, Break Rank and Follow, Make Ready, Fire, Fire at Will, Cease Fire, Charge.
[*]Voice phrases: Follow, Halt, Forward March, At the Double, Form Column, Single/Double Rank, Close Ranks, First/Second Rank Fire, Present Arms (square), Stand Ground (rally), Retreat.
[*]Chat orders: !form, !follow, !square, !ranks 1|2, !bayonet, !crouch, !pace, !music, !medic, !reinforce, !morale, !dismiss.
[*]Soldiers always walk to their place: no teleporting. In double rank, the front rank kneels.
[*]Morale: losses lower it, rallying (Stand Ground) restores it; a broken line falls back or routs.
[*][b]The colours hold the line:[/b] if you fall while your flag bearer still stands, the line halts and waits for you to be revived or to respawn by the flag. If both you and the flag bearer are down, the line breaks and disperses.
[/list]

[h2]Skirmishers[/h2]
[list]
[*]As an Infantry Officer, type [b]!skirm[/b]: 9 light infantry skirmishers.
[*]You place the line with the usual orders; they form in loose, irregular open order (one to two men apart) and kneel as soon as they reach their place.
[/list]

[h2]Cavalry squadrons[/h2]
[list]
[*]Spawn as [b]Hussar[/b] or [b]Dragoon[/b] and type [b]!line 8[/b]: your riders take your class and their horses.
[*]The squadron follows you on your track, matching your pace, and halts in double column.
[*]Charge (game order, voice phrase or Insult key): riders charge the enemy line you aimed at.
[*]!wedge (wedge charge), !skirmish (spread out and fire), !rally or Retreat (back behind you, even mid-charge).
[*]Riders who lose their horse keep fighting on foot.
[/list]

[h2]Artillery[/h2]
[list]
[*]Spawn as [b]Cannoneer[/b] and take a gun: 3 gun crew bots and 1 sapper arrive.
[*]You aim and fire; release the gun and the crew fetch a ball, load and ram it. They help you push the gun.
[*]The sapper digs earthworks in front of the gun and builds an ammo box next to it when you move far from the map's box.
[*]Optional: a crewman can lay and fire the gun himself when nobody holds it.
[/list]

[h2]Battle rules (optional)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Raise delay at round start, per-officer cap, per-round quota, server-wide bot cap scaled to the player count.
[*]Friendly-fire and fire-on-line discipline with progressive sanctions, general charge, artillery time windows, round announcements.
[*]AI lines for an empty side, balance bonus for the side with fewer players.
[/list]

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
[list]
[*]Add to your server config: [b]mods_installed_server_only 3830947372[/b]
[*]Works with default settings out of the box. Every setting is a [b]mod_variable CMD:<key>:<value>[/b] line per map rotation (full list in the mod's CONFIG documentation).
[*]Skirmishers use the Light Infantry class (or Rifleman): raise its spawn limit if needed. Sappers need the Sapper class allowed for the artillery side.
[*]Language: players get replies in the language of the commands they type (English or French). Default for announcements: [b]mod_variable CMD:language:en[/b] (or fr, or both).
[*]Admin chat command: [b]!cmd[/b] (state, on/off, caps, debug).
[*]Players: [b]!help[/b] lists the commands for your class, [b]!rules[/b] shows the server rules.
[/list]
